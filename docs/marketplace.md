# Invite-only freight marketplace

Businesses open **Load board** to post a shipment or use **Post this shipment to load board** in a shipment's detail panel. Posting includes an explicit review before sharing. Public-to-members fields are business name, city/state route, dates, equipment, cargo category, pallet count, weight, and a fixed offered rate or a request for private quotes. Exact street addresses and shipping contacts are stored separately and returned only to the posting business or its confirmed carrier. Keep personal information out of the business name and city fields.

Carriers open **Find loads**, search routes/businesses/cargo, filter equipment, and submit a reviewed quote or a fixed-rate booking request. **My requests** tracks submissions. The business reviews requests and explicitly confirms one carrier; accepting is a database transaction that rejects other pending requests and prevents double-booking. Confirmed bookings appear in the carrier's **My jobs**. Carrier contacts are not released to the business until confirmation. Competitors cannot see each other's quotes or contacts.

Businesses can close open posts or explicitly cancel booked ones. Carriers can withdraw pending requests while a post is open. Cancellation remains visible in request/job history and removes future carrier access to private shipping details. To change posted terms, close and repost. An offer revision is checked when accepting so a changed quote cannot be booked at stale displayed terms. Closing checks the displayed post state so closing an open listing cannot silently cancel a newly confirmed booking.

This board is shared PostgreSQL data, separate from the legacy workspace JSON. Existing sample loads remain under **Practice with sample loads**, with practice jobs and illustrative earnings separated from board bookings. New board bookings do not automatically dispatch freight, create payments, move money, upload proof, or populate a paid earnings ledger. GPS and shared pickup/delivery progression are not connected in this version. Carrier licensing/insurance and business identity beyond invited account membership are not automatically verified. This is a pilot coordination board, not a claim that a carrier is legally eligible or a delivery occurred.

## Database security

All marketplace tables and events are in `shypit_private`, with no direct anonymous/authenticated table access. Public RPCs require an active Shypit session permit and membership in the exact acting workspace. Business and carrier actions are role-restricted. The list RPC returns an explicit public-field projection and conditionally releases private details only after confirmation; business and carrier views are scoped independently even when one founder belongs to both workspaces. Membership or session revocation takes effect on every request.

Posting uses a workspace-bound request UUID and content fingerprint for retry safety. There is a 50-open-post cap per business and a 100-row board window, prioritizing the workspace's requests/bookings. Fixed-rate requests use the posted rate on the server; quote amounts are private. Booking, withdrawal, resubmission, and closure lock the same listing row to serialize state transitions. Accepted offer revisions and expected close states guard against stale browser tabs. No marketplace action charges a bank account or triggers an email/text.

Install through the existing authentication database activation workflow, which includes `supabase/marketplace.sql` and preserves existing data. No example listings are seeded in the live board. `scripts/test-marketplace.mjs` executes the SQL against PostgreSQL via PGlite: anonymous access, cross-workspace actions, private-data release, quote isolation, stale revisions, idempotency, fixed-rate integrity, withdrawal, cancellation, double booking, and revocation. Browser tests use two independent account views with a shared intercepted store to exercise posting, quote submission, booking, cancellation, search, fixed-price review, and mobile layout. Automated tests do not move real freight.
