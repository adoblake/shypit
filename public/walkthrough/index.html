Scripted demo with fictional accounts and sample data. It shows the current load-board booking flow. Stripe-hosted verification, live dispatch, delivery tracking, and payments are not performed in this recording.

Read the walkthrough
  1. Admin: Enter through Admin Login, review pilot counts, assign Overview-only access and separate company finance access, and inspect the audit history. Founder accounts remain protected.
  2. Business posting: Cedar Supply Co. posts eight pallets of office supplies from Phoenix to Harbor Office Goods in Los Angeles. The member listing shows the city route and cargo; exact addresses and contacts remain private.
  3. Carrier on mobile: Open Road Logistics LLC uses the compact sign-in, filters member loads, reviews the freight, and submits a private $920 quote. The business must confirm it before it becomes a booking.
  4. Business confirmation and banking: The shipper reviews and confirms the carrier. The selected parties gain access to booking contacts. Finance-authorized staff start test-mode payment-account setup in Billing; Stripe handles verification separately.
  5. Carrier jobs and banking: The carrier sees the confirmed job and private shipment details in My jobs, then reviews the test-mode payout setup in Earnings. No delivery, dispatch, or payout is completed.
