Admin controls, business posts, private quotes, shared bookings, and banking setup—on desktop and mobile.

Scripted demo with fictional accounts and sample data. It shows the current load-board booking flow. Stripe-hosted verification, live dispatch, delivery tracking, and payments are not performed in this recording.

Read the full text alternative (silent video)
  1. Admin: Enter through Admin Login, review pilot counts, assign Overview-only access and separate company finance access, and inspect the audit history. Founder accounts remain protected. The owner checks “Admin access enabled” and “Overview,” chooses “Review admin changes,” and confirms the reviewed permissions. “Grant finance access” gives the same teammate permission for their company workspace. Audit history shows both changes. Overview record counts are sample activity, not revenue or verified deliveries.
  2. Business posting: Cedar Supply Co. posts eight pallets of office supplies from Phoenix to Harbor Office Goods in Los Angeles. The member listing shows the city route and cargo; exact addresses and contacts remain private. The business chooses “Post a shipment,” enters the route, pickup and delivery dates, cargo, pallet count, weight, and equipment, then selects private quotes. A separate private section holds the pickup and delivery street addresses and shipping contact. “Review listing” presents a summary; “Publish to member load board” posts it for approved carrier members. A confirmation reports that the shipment is posted.
  3. Carrier on mobile: Open Road Logistics LLC uses the compact sign-in, filters member loads, reviews the freight, and submits a private $920 quote. The business must confirm it before it becomes a booking. The carrier signs in with its invited email and password, searches the route and equipment, opens the listing, and reviews its public details. Street addresses and shipping contacts are unavailable at this stage. The carrier enters the LLC name, $920 quote, and contact details, reviews the request, and submits it. The request is marked pending, not confirmed.
  4. Business confirmation and banking: The shipper reviews and confirms the carrier. The selected parties gain access to booking contacts. Finance-authorized staff start test-mode payment-account setup in Billing; Stripe handles verification separately. The business opens the shipment’s carrier requests, reviews the $920 private quote, and selects “Confirm carrier booking.” A confirmation prompt is accepted. The listing becomes booked and private addresses and contacts are available to the selected parties. Credentials and shipment terms still require separate verification. In Billing, “Add payment account” is the entry to hosted setup; the recording stops before external verification and charges.
  5. Carrier jobs and banking: The carrier sees the confirmed job and private shipment details in My jobs, then reviews the test-mode payout setup in Earnings. No delivery, dispatch, or payout is completed. In My jobs, the carrier opens the booked shipment and reads “Private booking details,” including pickup, delivery, and shipping contact. In Earnings, “Connect payout account” starts provider-hosted setup in a separate tab. Bank numbers belong on Stripe’s hosted page. Returning to Shypit checks provider status; an expired session can resume banking after sign-in. The finished example is post → private quote → confirmation → shared job.
